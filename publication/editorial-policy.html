The publication promise

Editorial & disclosure standards

Clarity and reproducible evidence matter more than dramatic headlines. Our articles distinguish educational examples, observations, candidate risks, and confirmed findings.

1. Research scope

We only describe testing performed on owned environments, public lab fixtures, or systems with explicit authorization. A static scanner observation is not evidence of exploitation.

2. Evidence and remediation

Research should disclose assumptions, reproducibility, sources, limitations, potential impact, and practical remediation. Claims are reviewed for accuracy before publication.

3. Responsible disclosure

No confidential vulnerability, third-party data, identifying target information, secrets, or exploit details are posted before the affected party authorizes disclosure and any embargo has passed. Active findings are reported privately.

4. Automated publication safeguards

Our publishing pipeline can prepare articles and social drafts, but does not auto-publish to social networks. New articles must be approved in an editorial review before their content is added to the public site, and Render deployment is a separate manual step.

5. Corrections

Material corrections are made transparently and reflected in the article. Contact the author through his public GitHub profile for non-sensitive editorial feedback. Use the affected project's private disclosure channel for security reports.
