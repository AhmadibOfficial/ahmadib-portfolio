# Ahmad-IB White Editorial Standard (v2)

Purpose: build international-facing, evidence-led cybersecurity publications. This is a creative standard, not a certification or guarantee of quality.

## Visual direction
- Dominant canvas: white #FFFFFF; pale paper #FAFBFD; navy ink #101B2E; secondary text #53647A.
- Accent: cobalt #1454E3 and pale blue #EEF4FF. No green backgrounds or green-themed graphic templates.
- Fonts: Inter / Inter Display where licensed or system-safe substitutes.
- Technical diagrams and reproducible reasoning over generic cyber stock imagery, exaggerated risk claims or decorative illustrations.
- One defensible thesis per slide, clear reading hierarchy, generous whitespace and meaningful captions.
- Explicit Ahmad-IB authorship and research-journal destination; do not use MetaAutomate company branding.
- Instagram carousels: 1080 × 1350, three or more coherent slides where appropriate.
- Vertical videos: 1080 × 1920; readable text and safe margins; describe silent drafts accurately. Narration and audio require separate approval.

## Content quality gates
1. Ground statements in published research or primary documentation; link to https://ahmadib.onrender.com and the precise supporting article.
2. Label exploratory observations separately from confirmed exploitability and demonstrated impact.
3. Use only test environments owned or authorized; keep unapproved third-party vulnerability details private.
4. Verify spelling, titles, claims, call-to-action links, and legibility before publication.
5. Keep Facebook excluded until page identity is verified and LinkedIn excluded until connected.
6. Under approved-auto, no post enters Metricool's auto-publish schedule without *individual* approval covering the exact caption, final media, target network, and local date/time.
7. Redesign approval is not publication approval. All first-batch items remain PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL until explicitly approved.
